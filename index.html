Orion-81

Orion-81 is a space combat game, still in development. It also has a purpose beyond play: its revenue is meant to help support software engineers and graphic designers whose work is being reshaped by artificial intelligence.

In development. Nothing to play or buy yet.

Why it exists

Artificial intelligence is changing how many kinds of work are done. The impacts of artificial intelligence on society bring real opportunities, and also change that some people feel sooner than others, software engineers and graphic designers among them.

Orion-81 is made with AI, openly, and directed by Andrius Bubliauskas. The idea behind it is that a project made this way can also play a small part in supporting people through that change. The plan is that the game's revenue will help support software engineers and graphic designers whose work has been affected by AI.

It is an early plan, not a promise of amounts. The game is not released, there is no revenue yet, and nothing has been paid. How the money will reach people is still to be worked out, and this site will say so when it is.

Sharing what AI gives

The thought is a simple one. As AI creates value, its benefits should be shared widely, including with the people whose work it changes most. Anthropic, whose Claude Code writes the code for this game, has described the same thought at a far larger scale: a public benefit fund, meant in part to support people through the economic impacts of AI.

Orion-81 is a very small project beside that. It comes from the same belief, applied to one game and the people closest to it.
