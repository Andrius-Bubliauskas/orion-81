Built with AI, end to end

Directed by Andrius Bubliauskas. Claude Code by Anthropic writes, plans and reviews the code.

Tested to the last rule

1,396 automated tests check the game's rules, and two different compilers give identical results.

A different kind of space combat

Six degrees of freedom: you move and turn freely along every axis. One heavy ship, its nose aimed with the mouse. Ships are machines, breaking along their seams.

Where it is going

Built so far: the rules core, the demo's rules, ships as machines and the engine-free part of first flight (cameras, controls, menus and a replay tool). Next: finishing game modes and balance, then air, orbit and descent, then the first flight on screen and the clean v0 demo.

Estimate of 19 October 2026: about 77 billion tokens of AI work to go, about 11 weeks at the current pace.
