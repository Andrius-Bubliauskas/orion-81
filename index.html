Orion-81

Orion-81 is a space combat game, still in development. It has a purpose beyond play: its revenue is meant to pay software engineers and graphic designers who lost their work to AI.

In development. Nothing to play or buy yet.

Why it exists

AI makes some kinds of work far cheaper to do, and the people who did that work pay the price. Software engineers and graphic designers were among the first to feel it, and some of them lost their livelihoods.

Orion-81 is made with AI, openly, and directed by Andrius Bubliauskas. The idea behind it is that a project made this way should give something back to the people whose work AI has displaced. So the plan is that the game's revenue goes to software engineers and graphic designers who lost their work to AI.

It is an early plan, not a promise of amounts. The game is not released, there is no revenue yet, and nothing has been paid. How the money will reach people is still to be worked out, and this site will say so when it is.

Sharing what AI gives

The thought is a simple one. When AI creates value, the people it affects should have a share of it, and the workers hit hardest should not be left to cope alone. Anthropic, whose Claude Code writes the code for this game, has described the same thought at a far larger scale: a public benefit fund, meant in part to help workers whose jobs AI disrupts.

Orion-81 is a very small project beside that. It comes from the same belief, applied to one game and the people closest to it.
