About this graph

This graph shows how Orion-81 was built, newest at the top: the game's commits and branches, with the Claude sessions, agents and workflows that did the work beside them. A coordinating session started the other sessions. A coloured badge on a commit links it to a session, and sometimes to an agent: a solid badge is matched from the work itself, a faded, italic one is inferred from timing, so treat those as good guesses. Type a date and time (for example 2026-10-18 21:30) to jump there and see what was running. Times are in your local time; tick UTC to switch. On a narrow screen, swipe the graph sideways to read the commits.
