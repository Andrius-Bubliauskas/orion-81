Legend and how to use
A chart of the work on Orion-81 since the restart on 14 October 2026, made from the project tracker and the game's history. Commit titles appear only where they are plain enough to publish; other commits show as "commit". Dashed shapes are estimates.
Wheel zooms around the pointer, drag pans (sideways in time, up and down through the lanes), a click opens an event in the side panel. Group headers collapse and expand. Two fingers pinch on a touch screen. Times are UTC.
Click any event on the chart to see its time, branch, work item and work area here.
